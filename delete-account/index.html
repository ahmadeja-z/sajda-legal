Delete Your Sajda Account

This page explains how you can request deletion of your Sajda account and associated personal data. You can use this page without logging into Sajda.

Current Deletion Method

Sajda does not currently include a self-service "Delete my account" button inside the app. To request deletion of your account and associated data, follow the manual request steps below and we will process your request.

  1. Send an account deletion request from the email address associated with your Sajda account to ahmadejaz2446@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line: Sajda Account Deletion Request.
  3. Include enough information to identify your account (such as the email or display name on your account), without sending passwords or authentication credentials.
  4. Account ownership may need to be verified before deletion can be completed.
  5. After verification, applicable account data will be deleted according to the Sajda Privacy Policy.

For your security, never include in a deletion request:

  • Your account password
  • A Google sign-in token
  • A Firebase authentication token
  • A one-time passcode (OTP)

What Account Deletion Affects

Based on Sajda's current functionality, deleting your account is expected to affect:

  • Your Firebase Authentication account (sign-in with email/password or Google).
  • Profile information associated with your account (email, display name, phone number if provided).
  • Your prayer completion and Qaza-e-Umri/Qasar tracking history stored in our database.
  • Any synced Quran reading progress associated with your account.
Note: The exact technical deletion process is still being finalized, and a specific data-retention period following deletion has not yet been defined. This page will be updated once that process and timeline are confirmed. We do not promise deletion of data beyond what is described in the Privacy Policy.

Questions

If you have questions about this process, contact us at ahmadejaz2446@gmail.com.
