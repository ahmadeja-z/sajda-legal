Sajda Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Sajda Islamic Application ("Sajda," "the app," "we," "us") collects, why, and how it is used. It covers the app's features, including Qaza-e-Umri tracking, prayer times, Azan reminders, the Quran reader, Qibla direction, Tasbih, Duas, and Hadith content.

1. Introduction

We respect your privacy and are committed to being transparent about the information Sajda collects and how it is used. This policy applies to anyone who uses the Sajda mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the app, we may collect the following:

Authentication Information

If you create an account, we collect your email address, a display name or username, and, for email-based sign-up, a phone number. If you sign in with Google, we receive the basic profile information Google provides for sign-in, such as your name, email address, and profile photo. Accounts are managed through Firebase Authentication.

Prayer and App Activity Data

We collect the prayer and Qaza-e-Umri (missed-prayer) tracking entries you record in the app, so your progress is saved and can be shown back to you across sessions and devices.

Location Permission

With your permission, Sajda reads your device's foreground location to calculate local prayer times and to determine the Qibla direction for your chosen calculation method. This is a device permission used to provide functionality — based on the app's current implementation, your location is used on your device for these calculations and is not written to our backend database. Sajda does not request or use background location access.

Camera Permission

Sajda may request camera access only while the Qibla screen's optional live camera (AR) view is open, to overlay the Qibla direction on the camera preview. The camera feed is used locally for this overlay and is not recorded or uploaded. If you prefer not to grant camera access, the compass-only Qibla view remains available.

Notifications

Sajda uses a push-notification token (via Firebase Cloud Messaging) and the device's local notification scheduler to deliver prayer-time and Azan reminders. You can enable or disable individual prayer reminders in the app's Azan settings.

Quran and Prayer Progress

Your prayer and Qaza/Qasar tracking data is stored in Firebase Cloud Firestore so it syncs across your sessions and devices. Quran content you read (translations, tafsir, recitation audio) is cached locally on your device to reduce repeated downloads; recitation audio is currently streamed rather than saved for offline playback.

Local Device Storage

Some preferences, such as your selected language and notification settings, are stored locally on your device rather than on our servers.

Support Messages

If you contact us through the app's Suggestions or Contact Us feature, we collect the name, email, subject, and message text you submit, so we can respond to you.

Analytics

Sajda does not currently use any analytics or crash-reporting SDK, and no advertising SDK is active. The app does not collect usage analytics or serve ads at this time.

3. How We Use Information

  • To create and maintain your account and sign you in.
  • To calculate your prayer times and Qibla direction for your location.
  • To save and display your prayer and Qaza tracking progress across sessions.
  • To deliver prayer-time and Qaza reminder notifications.
  • To respond to suggestions and support messages you send us.
  • To operate the app's core Islamic-content features (Quran, Duas, Hadith, Tasbih).

4. Third-Party Services

Sajda relies on the following third-party services to operate:

  • Firebase Authentication — manages account sign-in (email/password and Google Sign-In).
  • Google Cloud Firestore — stores account, tracking, and support-message data.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging — delivers push notifications to your device.
  • Google Sign-In — an optional sign-in method.
  • Quran content providers (such as the Al Quran Cloud API, the Tanzil Uthmani mushaf text, audio/CDN partners including islamic.network and mp3quran.net, and the tafsir_api project via jsDelivr) — deliver Quran text, translations, tafsir, and recitation audio. These are standard content-download requests; Sajda does not send your account or profile data to these providers.

Each provider processes data under its own privacy policy, which we encourage you to review if you have concerns about a specific provider.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account and tracking data for as long as your account remains active, so your prayer and Qaza progress persists across sessions and devices. Support messages are retained to allow us to review and respond to them.

Note: A specific retention period for data kept after account deletion has not yet been finalized. We will update this section once a defined retention period is confirmed.

6. Data Security

Account and tracking data is stored using Firebase's infrastructure, including Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore's built-in transport encryption. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Account and Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your Sajda account and associated data at any time. See the Account Deletion page for instructions.

8. Children's Privacy

Sajda is an Islamic application designed to support prayer, Qur'an reading, Qibla direction, Islamic learning, and other faith-based activities. The application may be used by individuals and families, including younger users under the guidance of a parent or legal guardian.

Sajda does not intentionally collect personal information from children beyond what is necessary to provide the application's features. Features that involve creating an account, saving personal progress, prayer history, or synchronizing data should be used by children with the knowledge and supervision of a parent or guardian where required by applicable law.

Sajda does not contain advertising targeted at children.
